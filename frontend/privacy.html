Privacy Policy

Institutional Data Protection Node Active

Sparc Energy Pvt. Ltd. ("Sparc Energy," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the integrity of institutional data and complying with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 of India.

1. Information Governance

We collect personal and professional information essential for institutional carbon market participation. This includes identity verification documents (PAN/Aadhaar/GSTN), corporate registration data, and secure financial transaction records.

2. Strategic Use of Data

Data is utilized strictly for KYC compliance, trade settlement, registry document management, and generating auditable retirement certificates compatible with international standards.

3. Advanced Security Protocols

We implement bank-grade encryption and decentralized storage solutions (Supabase/Mega) to ensure the confidentiality of sensitive documents. All metadata associated with carbon credit retirement is cryptographically linked to ensure non-repudiation.
